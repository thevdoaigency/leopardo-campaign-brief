02 · Color

Palette

The palette inherits Leopardo Blue as the primary anchor, so GATOR sits comfortably alongside Leopardo Construction and Leopardo Efficiency. 05 · Tone

Voice Attributes

GATOR sounds like a senior partner inside a construction firm who has spent thirty years working with municipalities — not a startup engineer. It carries the weight of Leopardo's relationships into every word.

Established
Anchored in decades of municipal and construction experience. GATOR is not a new technology built by people learning government — it is a tool built by people who already know.
Specific
Real city names. Real contract values. Real savings percentages. No generic claims, no vague promises.
Direct
Lead with what the platform does and what it returns. No preamble. No buzzwords.
Quiet
No exclamation. No hype. Procurement directors and finance managers don't buy from loud rooms.
06 · Application

In Use

Real-world artifacts: business card, case study card, social card, RFP cover. Each one signals GATOR is part of the Leopardo family — same fonts, same blue, same professional weight.
