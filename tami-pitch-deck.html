04 · The Cost of Another Rep
GATOR
A Leopardo Technology

Another rep is the right answer — at a later stage.

Right now, the bottleneck isn't how many reps. It's what's behind each rep. Adding a second salesperson doesn't fix it, and adds three new costs.

Reason 01
Territory Split
A second rep takes accounts you would have closed. Florida, Georgia, and South Carolina are your relationships. Splitting them halves the leverage of the network you already built.
Reason 02
Duplicated Overhead
They build the same decks, the same RFP responses, the same case studies you build. Twice the wasted hours. Twice the inconsistency in what gets sent to procurement directors.
Reason 03
No Leverage Compounding
A rep produces sales. A support function produces sales and assets that work forever. One case study sells the next ten cities. One RFP template wins back hundreds of hours.
A second rep is linear. Support behind one rep is exponential.

Executive Summary

The City of [Municipality] has identified a common but expensive condition: forty-plus departments procuring independently, with no aggregated leverage, no centralized vendor competition, and an estimated 20–35 percent premium on procurement spend relative to consolidated purchasing models.

GATOR was built to eliminate that premium. The platform aggregates departmental orders across a municipality and submits the combined volume to a blind reverse auction with qualified vendors. Average procurement savings across our first five deployments: 27 percent in year one.

About GATOR

GATOR is the procurement technology platform of the Leopardo family of companies — Leopardo Construction (1977) and Leopardo Efficiency (energy savings performance contracts for municipal governments since 2008). The platform was built by operators who have spent decades inside municipal procurement environments as contractors, consultants, and service providers.
