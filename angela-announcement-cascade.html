1 Angela's post — three to choose from

All three are first-person, short enough to read in one glance, and stay at the Leopardo Efficiency level. Whichever Angela picks, she has to be able to carry it in the comments — the durable post is the one she'll still sound like a week later.

A
Closest to home
Lowest risk · warms up her existing voice
Safe
After years on the economic development side of govtech, I've joined Leopardo Efficiency to lead business development. Same belief that's driven my whole career: government works better when the people inside it have better tools. Glad to be doing it with this team. If we've crossed paths in economic development or local government, let's stay connected.
What it signals: She wants to stay where it's comfortable. Reads clean, asks nothing of her she hasn't already done. The floor, not the ceiling.
B
The turn
The brief's target energy · a quiet pivot, ending on a question
Recommended
For 20 years I've worked the space between government and the technology that's supposed to make it run better. I just joined Leopardo Efficiency to take that on from a new angle: procurement. Government spends enormous sums buying goods and services, often through processes older than the people running them. That's the part I want to fix now. If you work in local government or procurement, I'd like to hear how it actually looks from your seat.
What it signals: A real pivot from her old world (economic development relationships) to the new one (procurement). Ends on a question instead of a pitch — which suits a relationships person and invites her network in without asking for anything.
C
Most of herself
A real point of view · gestures at why the work matters
Bold
I've spent my career helping government work better, and here's the part nobody posts about: the win was never the technology. It's what changes for the person on the other end of it — the resident, the small business, the town that finally gets something it waited years for. I've joined Leopardo Efficiency to work on procurement, where a lot of that quiet difference is won or lost. It's the most direct version of the work I've always cared about. If that's your world too, reach out — I'd rather build this with people than at them.
What it signals: The only version that says why the work matters — the people on the far end of it. Sounds least like a press release, most like a person. If she reaches for this, there's more home than the scripts suggest.
Two dials only Angela can set
The anchor number. "20 years" is a placeholder — make it the real figure, or swap it for the thing she's actually known for (CivicServe, the economic-development relationships, the govtech arc).

Name GATOR, or keep it general. Options B and C can name the procurement platform specifically if she wants to be concrete, or stay at the Leopardo Efficiency level. Her call.
