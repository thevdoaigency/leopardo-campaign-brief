5 Why the cadence works

Staggering beats front-loading. Angela's post reaches public-sector practitioners who already trust her. The company repost adds algorithmic reach. Curtis's repost reaches an overlapping but different network. Rob's engagement signals internal alignment. Each layer lands on a different day, so the announcement keeps resurfacing instead of spiking once and disappearing — and each one transfers a little of Angela's credibility to Leopardo Efficiency while opening warm doors across her network.
The one rule on every post: the words can be sharpened, but we only ever write the emotional truth. Whatever goes out under someone's name has to be something they'd say the same way if asked cold in a room. Angela's post clears that bar — it's hers.
