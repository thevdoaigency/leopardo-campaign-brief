How It Works

Three steps. No data entry. No bridge to maintain.

DFH Connect reads the XML output from Emullion, transforms it into the formats QuickBooks Enterprise expects, and creates the corresponding records automatically. The team doesn't change tools. The data just stops requiring a human translator.

01

Emullion completes the estimate

Your team works in Emullion the same way they do today. When the estimate is finalized, it generates the standard XML export.

02

DFH Connect translates and validates

The platform processes the XML, maps line items to existing inventory, and adds new SKUs when needed. Nothing is skipped or guessed.

03

QuickBooks receives the records

Sales orders, purchase orders, invoices, and inventory updates appear in QuickBooks Enterprise — ready for billing, fulfillment, and reconciliation.
