01 · The Delta

What was contracted. What was built.

Contract — $3,000 / month

  • 1 hero video per month, 2 minutes, polished.
  • 5 cutdowns per month — LinkedIn landscape and Instagram vertical, captioned.
  • Pipeline management — Monday.com organization, LucidLink hosting, distributed editing team.

Delivered

  • Everything in the contract. Hero videos, cutdowns, pipeline.
  • 199 researched city decision-makers. Ohio mayors, city managers, finance directors. A Florida list staged behind the Ohio wave.
  • 40-post 8-week campaign architecture across 5 channels — LinkedIn Company, Curtis personal, Rob personal, Instagram, YouTube.
  • LinkedIn profile work for Curtis and Rob — repositioned to anchor the campaign narrative.
  • Voice-clone production system — Curtis and Rob VOs deployable on b-roll without scheduling either of them on camera.
  • Custom graphic builder. A team tool that produces brand-locked title cards, stat cards, and quote graphics on demand — every asset matches the documented visual spec without round-tripping through design.
  • Automated client reporting. Curtis gets a clean weekly email — what was delivered, what's coming, direct links to every video asset. No status meetings required.
  • Outreach engine — connection-request workflow, status tracking, LinkedIn-to-Monday pipeline.
  • Brand-locked production system — every asset built to a documented visual and voice spec.
